## Discovered

### Confirmed — candidate deep-decode

| Candidate | ~Size | `+0x61` | `+0x98` | Queue edges | Notable strings / role |
|-----------|-------|---------|---------|-------------|------------------------|
| `0x519E20` | 8028 B | yes (cmp/clear) | no | **none** to enqueue/workers/lookup/deferred | `"forge"`, `"Data{}.{}"`, allocator errors; calls `0x426EE50` |
| `0x2D4F30` | 5992 B | yes (writes 0) | no | **none** | **`ClothRagdollObject`** (repeated) — **not** Edward/queue subject |
| `0x1D9CC0` | 5820 B | yes (reads) | mixed* | **none** | SmallArray overflow strings only |

\*Several `+0x98` hits are stack `[rsp+98]`; real object loads include `[r9+98]` / `[rdx+98]`.

**Cross-links:** no candidate calls another (except `0x519E20` self-recursion sites). **No** direct call to `0x2E97B60`, `0x2E97FC0`, `0x725400`, `0xE95E80`, `0x1AC380`, or `0x429F9E0`.

**Callers:** each has a small E8 caller set; none identified as the deferred-queue producer path.

**Verdict:** v0.6.8 fingerprint top-hits are **not** proven subject constructors for the deferred queue. `0x2D4F30` is a **ClothRagdollObject** path (false positive for Edward).

### Confirmed — LOD comparator / thunk consumers

| Target | E8 callers | LEA consumers | Unique funcs (sample cap) |
|--------|------------|---------------|---------------------------|
| `0x67BDB30` | **0** | many (capped 60) | **22** |
| `0x1A5A00` | **0** | many (capped 60) | **22** |

- Consumers install the addresses via **LEA + store** into callback/bundle slots (typical `+0x8/+0x10/+0x18/+0x20` patterns), not via direct calls.
- Phase registrar still LEAs both into actor phases, including **`UpdateActorLOD`** (`0x5CBE039` → cmp, `0x5CBE059` → thunk, `0x5CBE045` → stub `0x1A5A70`).
- Same cmp/thunk pair is reused across many actor-related bundle initializers — a **shared comparison/dispatch primitive**, not an Edward factory.

### Hypothesis (not verified)

- `0x519E20` may relate to forge/data object bookkeeping that happens to share a `+0x61` flag byte layout.
- True queue-subject constructors likely lie on paths that eventually reach wrapper `0x2E97FC0` / nested `+0x18→+0xA8→+0x3CD0`, not the ClothRagdoll/forge false positives.
