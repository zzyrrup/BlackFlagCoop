## Why

1. **Last-def** for Begin `(ecx=1,edx=0)` and Dup `(edx=0,r8=0)`
2. **Freestanding End** (no rcx/arg setup)
3. Prefer Begin leaves that **arm TLS / RIP-rel globals** and consume **both cl and dl**
