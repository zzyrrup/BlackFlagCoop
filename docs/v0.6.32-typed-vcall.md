### Confirmed — upstream owners of Neighbor bridge

| Owner | edx | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| `0x6193380` | 3 | Primary edx=3 → `[vt+0x110]` |
| `0x61934C0` | 2 | Primary edx=2 → `[vt+0x110]` |
| Cluster siblings | 2/3/4/5… | Same pattern, table-published |

**Shared pattern:**

1. `GateHelper 0x4DEAC50` (hash-gate).
2. `LookupHelper 0x4DBF820` → object.
3. `mov r8,[obj]; mov edx,2|3; call [r8+0x110]` → Neighbor contract.

Published in `.edata` method table around **`0x9242FE8`** (E8 callers of owners = **0** → table-dispatched).

**Note:** `lea r8,[rsi+18]` in the edx=2 owner is a **float-field address** passed to vcall `+0x118`, **not** Neighbor’s nested-object `mov rcx,[this+18]`.

### Nest `+0x18`

| Idiom | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Neighbor `mov rcx,[this+18]` then `[rcx+98]` | Nested **pointer-family** object |
| Owner `lea r8,[this+18]` | Field arg to `+0x118` |
| Generic `mov [reg+18]` → `call [reg+20]` | Common untyped idiom (not NS-specific offline) |
