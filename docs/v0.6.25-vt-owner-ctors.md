### Confirmed — constructors of owner objects

These are **in-place ctors**: `lea vt; mov [rcx], vt; … init …`.  
**All have 0 E8 callers** offline — published as `VT[0]` and/or secondary tables; construction is table/TypeInfo-dispatched, not a direct `E8` Alloc+ctor site.

| Family | Ctor | Evidence |
|--------|------|----------|
| Neighbor | `0x13612B0` | `VT[0]`; sets vptr `0x976B2D0` |
| Neighbor | `0x3D522C0` | Also LEAs `0x976B2D0`; inits `+0x40…` transforms; **`mov [rcx+98], 0`**; published `@0xAAD22E0` |
| Spatial | `0x5781490` | `VT[0]`; **`add rcx, 98`** then side-init call |
| Spatial | `0x5563CF0` | Calls helper `0x5563D30`; LEAs spat VT; **`add rsi, 98`**; jmp init |
| Spatial | `0x6CF34A0` | Same spat VT + `+0x98` advance pattern |
| Spatial | `0x6CF3750` | Calls helper; **stores into `[rsi+98]`** |

**Confirmed:** “who constructs” these owners = the above ctors (especially `VT[0]`).  
**Not found:** a unique offline `Allocator → E8 ctor` wrapper for these specific installers (0 E8 to them).

### Confirmed — enrollment / `+0x98` relation

| Link | Evidence |
|------|----------|
| Spatial ctors touch `+0x98` | `add reg, 98` then init (`0x5781490`, `0x5563CF0`, `0x6CF34A0`); store `[rsi+98]` (`0x6CF3750`) |
| Neighbor-family has `+0x98` field | `0x3D522C0` zeros `[rcx+98]` at construct |
| Same offset as enrollment side-block | Prior: `0xE95E80` allocates 0x160 and stores at `object+0x98` |

**Confirmed:** owner objects of neighbor/spatial methods are the same structural family that carries the enrollment/arming **`+0x98`** side-block field.

### Arming

Arming `0x21085D0` / wrap `0x2108620` / gate `0x53F31A0` have **0 E8** from these ctor bodies (they are themselves table-dispatched). No same-function call edge ctor→arming offline.

**Hypothesis:** after `0xE95E80` attaches the side-block at `+0x98`, arming runs on these same owner instances (not newly proven this milestone).
